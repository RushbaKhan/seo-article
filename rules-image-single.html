SEO Department — Official Rules

Every employee must complete 1 fully finished website every week.

Website Standards

Every website must have:

  • Unique content (No copy-paste)
  • Title tags & meta descriptions
  • sitemap.xml & robots.txt
  • Mobile responsive design
  • Internal linking
  • Game-specific branding & images
  • Optimized image alt text
  • Perfect SEO (80 or 80+) — checked on SEOBILITY

Voice Channel (VC) Rules

  • 6 hours in VC every working day (Mandatory)
  • All work must be completed while in VC
  • Building, keyword research, deployments, GitHub, SEO tasks — all during VC
  • No AFK or idle time in VC
  • Complete your full VC requirement every day

Before Marking a Website Complete

Make sure:

  • Website works on desktop & mobile
  • SSL (HTTPS) is active
  • Google Search Console is verified
  • Sitemap submitted
  • Internal links working
  • Unique content
  • No deployment or SEO issues

Rejected / Failed Websites

  • Fix and resubmit the same day
  • Do not leave broken or incomplete websites overnight

Website Ownership & Access

  • Once completed, site access remains with the manager
  • You cannot delete the site — even on resignation or leaving

Leave Policy

  • No paid leaves
  • Maximum 3 unpaid leaves per month
  • Inform manager before start of day
  • Valid reason required for any leave

Device Policy

  • Work only on PC/Laptop
  • No working or joining VC from mobile

Communication

  • Report all issues to direct manager only
  • Do not contact upper management directly

Meetings

  • Attendance is mandatory
  • Be on time
  • Can't attend → inform manager beforehand

Team Rules

  • Help each other
  • No gatekeeping knowledge, templates, or workflows
  • Don't interfere with others' domains, repos, or websites
  • No unnecessary distractions or trolling in VC

Domain Rules

  • Keyword research before purchasing any domain
  • All domains purchased through Cloudflare
  • One Domain = One Website = One GitHub Repo = One Cloudflare Project
  • Do not waste or abandon domains

Ownership & Access

  • All sites, domains, repos, Cloudflare & GSC belong to ZADEYO
  • Do not delete sites or domains under any circumstances
  • Never share department info outside the team
  • CURSOR PRO — company SEO work only; regular checkups by manager

A Website Is Complete Only When

  • Keyword research completed
  • Domain purchased
  • Website built with unique content
  • GitHub updated
  • Deployed on Cloudflare Pages
  • Google Search Console verified
  • Sitemap submitted
  • Indexing requested
